Within-person cohort comparisons show association of radiographic OA and pain, not interchangeability of structural grade and symptoms. Do not incorrectly claim there is no association.

## Six seed propositions

- **cartilage.classification_context**: ICRS describes articular cartilage lesion structure and location, while patient symptoms and function are recorded separately. Record classification system, assessed structure, method and examiner; do not infer a structural grade from pain. Source 1; descriptive consensus/instrument; no clinical sensitivity/specificity established here.
- **cartilage.history_exam**: Cartilage evaluation can document onset, injury context, swelling, catching/locking, functional limitation, prior surgery, effusion and motion. These entries are assessment domains, not diagnostic criteria proving a cartilage lesion. Source 1; instrument, not diagnostic-accuracy study.
- **osteochondral.mri_context**: AAOS 2023 supports considering MRI to characterize a known knee OCD lesion on radiographs or suspected concomitant pathology. Recommendation strength High. Source 2; population specifically knee OCD, not all knee pain or every cartilage abnormality.
- **osteochondral.specialist_assessment**: For symptomatic unstable/displaced knee OCD lesions, the 2023 guideline offers surgery as an option in both skeletally immature and mature patients, with Limited recommendation strength. Document maturity, stability/displacement and symptoms for specialist review; no automatic procedure choice. Source 2.
- **osteochondral.follow_up**: Persistent symptoms after OCD treatment justify considering repeat history, examination and radiographs and/or MRI to assess healing; this is AAOS consensus, not high-quality comparative treatment evidence. Source 2.
- **oa.radiograph_symptom_separation**: Kellgren–Lawrence is a radiographic OA grade. Record pain and function separately even though structural severity and knee pain can be associated; no deterministic symptom, disability or treatment mapping should be inferred from KL alone. Sources 3–4; radiographic classification plus observational association.
