## Proposed Draft propositions (15; cartilage supplement adds 6)

1. **oa.exercise** — ACR/AF 2019 strongly supports exercise for knee OA; activity choice should account for preference, access and individual circumstances. Source acr_oa_2019; guideline recommendation, not individual response prediction.
2. **oa.weight_management** — For knee OA with overweight or obesity, ACR/AF 2019 strongly supports weight loss. Source acr_oa_2019; no automatic targets or protocol inferred.
3. **oa.topical_nsaid** — Topical NSAIDs are strongly supported in knee OA in ACR/AF 2019. Comorbidities and medication risks still require assessment. Source acr_oa_2019; no dose/automatic prescribing.
4. **oa.presentation** — OA can involve pain, stiffness, swelling and reduced joint function; these findings are not individually diagnostic. Source acr_oa_2019; descriptive background.
5. **oa.prp_aaos** — AAOS 2021 describes possible PRP pain/function benefit for symptomatic knee OA, with Limited recommendation strength. Source aaos_oa_2021; preparation variation and evidence limits remain relevant.
6. **oa.prp_acr** — ACR/AF 2019 strongly recommends against PRP for knee/hip OA, citing preparation and technique heterogeneity. Source acr_oa_2019. Record explicitly as disagreement with aaos_oa_2021, without selecting a preferred recommendation.
7. **oa.ha_aaos** — AAOS 2021 advises against routine HA injection use for symptomatic knee OA, with Moderate recommendation strength. Source aaos_oa_2021; regulatory authorization is a separate field.
8. **meniscus.scope** — The AAOS 2024 acute isolated meniscal guideline does not cover degenerative tears or associated ligament injury. Source aaos_meniscus_2024; record injury onset/mechanism and associated pathology before applying its content.
9. **meniscus.examination** — Joint-line tenderness and meniscal maneuvers can inform assessment, but diagnostic accuracy varies across tests and studies. Source aaos_meniscus_2024; no universal sensitivity/specificity assigned.
10. **meniscus.mri** — AAOS 2024 identifies MRI as the preferred modality for suspected acute meniscal tears (Strong recommendation, High evidence quality). Source aaos_meniscus_2024; no automatic MRI order or interpretation.
11. **meniscus.motion_block** — AAOS 2024 consensus highlights displaced acute tears restricting movement as potential candidates for acute specialist surgical assessment. Source aaos_meniscus_2024; Very Low evidence quality, Consensus option, not a confirmed tear from reported locking.
12. **meniscus.degenerative_rehab** — ESCAPE found exercise-based physical therapy noninferior to partial meniscectomy for five-year knee function in its enrolled degenerative-tear population. Source escape_2022; exclusions, crossover and noninferiority design limit extrapolation.
13. **safety.hot_joint** — SANJO calls for suspicion with a painful inflamed joint, with or without fever; clinical findings alone cannot confirm or exclude infection. Source sanjo_2023, B1. Draft safety knowledge only; not a newly activated safety-engine rule.
14. **safety.sepsis** — SANJO identifies concomitant sepsis/shock as requiring immediate clinical attention. Source sanjo_2023, B2. Synthetic review scenarios must not emit a confirmed diagnosis.
15. **regulatory.cell_exosome_scope** — The FDA 2020 notice states the listed stem-cell, exosome and adipose-derived/SVF products lacked orthopedic-condition approval. Source fda_regen_2020; date- and US-specific, separate from efficacy and current product authorization.

## Orthobiologic research records to keep distinct

- **ACS**: acs_review_2024 reports conflicting comparisons, high heterogeneity and insufficient support; saline comparisons did not establish six-month pain/function benefit. Research evidence only, no approval or guideline endorsement inferred. Abstract-only review limits this extraction.
- **PRGF**: prgf_rct_2017 compared one preparation against HA for six months; between-group symptom/function differences were not significant. Absence of significance does not prove equivalence, efficacy over placebo, cartilage regeneration or preparation interchangeability.
- **PL**: pl_review_2024 abstract finds sparse clinical literature and calls for larger comparative studies. Do not encode its optimistic wording as established safety/efficacy. No numeric effect extracted.
- **PRP / HA**: keep AAOS and ACR recommendations separately with source edition and explicit conflict. Guidance strength, effect magnitude and regulatory status are different properties.
- **Exosomes / SVF / MSCs**: fda_regen_2020 is regulatory/safety material, not efficacy evidence. The ACR 2019 stem-cell recommendation should not be treated as a complete review of all modern products. Mechanism, product, manufacturing and jurisdiction need separate specialist review; current product-specific status unknown in this seed.

ACS, PRGF, PL, PRP, HA, exosomes, SVF and MSCs should each have a separate treatment record. Unknown contraindications, effect size, adverse-event rate, duration and regulatory status remain explicitly unknown pending extraction. Clinic experience/outcomes are not sources of efficacy recommendations.
